# Sounds – sources & licenses (Pumpkin Carver)

All sounds are recorded samples released under **CC0 1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No synth beeps. All were reused unchanged from earlier games in this Halloween series (already trimmed, EQ'd and loudness-normalized there). `tools/build.mjs` embeds them as base64 into `js/sounds-src.js` so the game works via `file://`. Credit is not required for CC0 and is given voluntarily.
